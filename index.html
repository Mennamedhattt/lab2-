Where this shows up

The same handful of formulas, applied everywhere pixels need reshaping.

Medical imaging

Negative and gray-level slicing pull faint structures out of dark regions on X-rays and CT scans.

Remote sensing

Contrast stretching rescues satellite images shot under poor illumination or sensor limits.

Display calibration

Every screen applies its own gamma correction — power-law transforms compensate for it.

Computer vision

Thresholding and histograms are the first step before edge detection, segmentation, or OCR.
